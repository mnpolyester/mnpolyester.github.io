About M.N. Polyester

M.N. Polyester (India) Pvt. Ltd. has served the South Indian composites industry since 2005. Based in Coimbatore, we manufacture and supply unsaturated polyester resins and gelcoats.

Our range includes general purpose and isophthalic resins, gelcoats and speciality grades, with UV-stabilised options. Explore the product range, review the grade documents, or contact our team about your application.

Our Products

Resins and gelcoats for composites manufacturing, with grade-specific technical documents available below.
